Questions

Frequently asked

Where do you build?

We're based in Tacoma and work throughout the South Sound — including University Place, Gig Harbor, Bremerton, Puyallup, and the surrounding Pacific Northwest.

Do you take on both remodels and new builds?

Yes. We handle full-scale renovations, historic restorations, ground-up new construction, and ADUs/DADUs. The bigger the challenge, the better.

Can you help with design?

Absolutely. Alexis leads design direction to bring your vision to life, and we coordinate architectural plans and permitting so you don't have to.

How do we get started?

Reach out through our contact page with as much detail as you can about your project. We'll follow up to learn more and map out next steps.
