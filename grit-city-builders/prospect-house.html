Prospect House

A 1904 home in North End Tacoma, reborn.

The Project

From downtrodden to Craftorian

We turned this downtrodden 1904 house — 4 bedrooms and 1 bath — into a gorgeous Craftorian-inspired home with 4 bedrooms, 3 baths, and a brand-new detached garage.

We relocated the staircase to give it the moment it deserves, fully fenced the back yard to create a private oasis, and even built a bocce ball court for our clients. Enjoy the before-and-afters as much as we enjoyed the work.
